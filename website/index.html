Installing the Firmware

requirements:

  • Official latest micropython Firmware
  • a pi pico or any supported board with GPIO pins
  • a usb cable for connecting the board to the PC

Setup:

  1. Go tho the official link and click on the option for your board I will use:

    2. pi pico 2 w

  2. Download micropython for your PICO BOARD

  3. plug the USB cable to the PC and then while pressing the bootsel button plug the cable to the board(pico)

    4. note: this will allow the board to appear the board as a USB STORAGE device

  4. Drag and drop the Firmware you downloaded into the device that showed in the pc(the name you will see depends on the board)

If you don't see RPI-RP2 or RP2350,or whataver name your board is, common causes are:

  • A charge-only USB cable (use a data cable).
  • Not holding BOOTSEL before plugging in the USB.
  • A USB port or driver issue on the computer.
